02 / Redesign recommendations

03 / Proposed data model

Separate grains, conformed meaning, reproducible briefings

Transactions, quarterly plans, invoices, KPI thresholds, and executive snapshots cannot safely live at one grain. The model preserves each fact and joins them through governed dimensions.

Grain first

Actual transactions roll to quarter before comparison with quarterly budget. No direct transaction-to-target join is permitted.

Ratios stay non-additive

Gross and net margin are recomputed from summed numerators and denominators. They are never summed or averaged.

Cycles are immutable

Each publication pins data, plan, metric, commentary, and calculation versions so a past briefing can be reproduced.

Receivables need snapshots

Cumulative paid amounts cannot recreate historical aging. Payment events and governed as-of snapshots are production requirements.

04 / Data flow specification

Validate daily. Freeze at close. Publish by version.

The operational pipeline can refresh frequently, but the executive briefing should change only through a controlled reporting-cycle publication.

Source landing

Versioned publication
  • Quality gateSix paid-before-invoice records are quarantined from aging and payment-timeliness measures. They are not silently corrected.
  • Reconciliation gateThe variance bridge must start at budget net income and finish at certified actual net income within $0.01.
  • Semantic gateDirectionality is governed by metric and P&L line; lower expense is favorable even when raw arithmetic signs are negative.
  • Publication gateLate corrections create a new restated reporting-cycle version and preserve the prior briefing.
  • Narrative gatePublished commentary must reference the same metric, plan, cycle, and calculation versions visible to the reader.

Delivery roadmap

Build trust before adding breadth

The fastest credible path starts with a reconciled P&L semantic layer, then adds receivables and forward-looking evidence only when their source controls are ready.

Phase 1

Govern the close

Confirm fiscal calendar, scope, currency, signs, plan version, metric definitions, materiality, and certification roles.

Phase 2

Ship the P&L brief

Build actual versus budget, margin measures, the reconciled bridge, immutable snapshots, and finance detail.

Phase 3

Repair collections evidence

Resolve date anomalies, source payment events, define the cohort and as-of rules, then certify AR snapshots.

Phase 4

Add outlook

Backfill comparable actuals and integrate approved forecast/scenario data before adding trajectory claims.

Acceptance test: In under one minute, a leader should be able to state the material P&L result, its two largest drivers, the collections implication, and the limits of the evidence without opening a filter or decoding a chart.
